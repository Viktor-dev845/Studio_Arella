## 1. Introduction to the Dashboard
When you log in to the platform, you will land on your **Dashboard**. 

- **Overview:** The Dashboard provides a quick summary of your active campaigns, upcoming bookings, and general account statistics.
- **Navigation Menu:** On the left sidebar, you can navigate to critical sections of the app including **Creatives (Ads)**, **Bookings**, **Cart**, and **Analytics**.

---

## 2. Step 1: Uploading & Managing Your Ads (Creatives)
Before you can book a slot on the billboard, you must first upload the image or video you want to display.

1. Navigate to the **Creatives** section from the sidebar.
2. Click on **Upload Ad** or **Create Creative**.
3. Fill in the required details (Name, Description) and upload your file (Video or Image).
4. **Important Note on Approval:** All uploaded videos and images go through an automated AI moderation process to ensure they meet our content policies. Your ad will initially show a status of `Pending`. Once approved, it will become available for booking.

---

## 3. Step 2: Scheduling & Booking Slots
Once your creative is approved, you are ready to secure your airtime. Navigate to the **Book** section.

### Selecting Your Ad
The system will display a list of your approved creatives. Click on the ad you want to schedule.

### Picking a Date
A calendar will be displayed. Click on the specific day you wish to book.

### Selecting Hours
- The LED screen operates strictly between **6:00 AM and 7:00 PM (West Africa Time)**. 
- Click on the hour blocks you wish to book (e.g., `7:00 AM`, `8:00 AM`, `10:00 AM`).
- **Auto-Selection:** The system will intelligently and automatically pick the next available free minute within your selected hours so you don't have to manually search for empty gaps!

### Duration & Loops (Full Plays)
- You can adjust how many times you want your video to loop back-to-back within that selected time block using the `+` and `-` buttons. 
- The system will calculate the total time needed and instantly display the calculated price.

### Spread Booking (Recurring Ads)
If you want your ad to run on a recurring schedule without clicking the calendar manually every day:
1. Click the **Spread Booking...** button.
2. Choose a duration (e.g., `1 Week`, `4 Weeks`, `3 Months`).
3. Select your desired pattern (e.g., `Every Day`, `Weekdays`, `Weekends`).
4. The system will automatically clone your selected hours across the entire chosen period!

### Add to Cart
Once you are satisfied with your schedule, click the **Add to Cart** button. Your selected slots will be temporarily reserved for your session and highlighted in gold.

---

## 4. Step 3: Cart & Checkout
1. Click on **View Cart** or navigate to the **Cart** page via the sidebar.
2. Here, you will see a summary of all the slots you've selected, along with the total cost.
3. Click **Checkout**. 
4. **Reservation Timer:** Upon clicking checkout, your slots are strictly locked for you. You have exactly **5 minutes** to complete the payment. If payment is not completed in this window, the slots will be released back to the public for others to book.
5. Complete your payment via the provided payment gateway to finalize your booking!

---

## 5. Analytics & Reporting
After your ad has played on the physical screen, you can track its performance.

- Navigate to the **Analytics** page.
- You can view exactly how many times your ad played.
- **Impressions Tracking:** The system utilizes an advanced mathematical model to calculate estimated viewer impressions based on the time of day your ad played (e.g., Rush hour slots between 7 AM - 9 AM generate significantly higher impressions than mid-day slots).
